How to read this

Win rate = ads that ever won (current Winners + past winners) ÷ graded ads (Winner, On the fence or Loser). Testing and unspent ads aren't counted. It's the same all-time win rate as the client dashboards and the pod page, and it counts every graded ad, old tracker included.

Older ads from before the CID tracker have no awareness level, lead type, angle, idea or talent recorded, so in those tables they sit in one Old tracker (not tagged) row — each table still adds up to the client's baseline. Static vs video and net new vs iteration include them.

A value needs 8+ graded ads for its own row (smaller ones are pooled into "Other"); under 5 ads only the count is shown. Personas are inferred: the trackers don't record one yet, so each ad gets a persona from its Idea and Angle names using keyword rules (e.g. "DIY Moves Cost More" → DIY-considering mover). Treat it as a guide; it gets replaced by Nico's persona mapping and a tracker Persona column once those exist. Spend is Meta spend on the graded ads; Top spender is the highest-spend ad in that row.
