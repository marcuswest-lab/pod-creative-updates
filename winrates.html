How to read this

Win rate = ads that ever won (current Winners + past winners) ÷ graded ads (Winner, On the fence or Loser). Testing and unspent ads aren't counted. Same all-time win rate as the client dashboards and the pod page.

Win score = win rate × average spend per winner (it works out to total spend on winners ÷ ads tested, i.e. winning dollars produced per ad we test). It measures how big the winners are as well as how often we hit: a category that wins less often but produces big winners can beat one that wins often but small. Spend is lifetime Meta spend, so older winners have had more time to spend.

Personas are inferred from each ad's Idea and Angle names using keyword rules (e.g. "DIY Moves Cost More" → DIY-considering mover) until Nico's persona mapping and a tracker Persona column exist.

Ads from before the CID tracker have no awareness, lead type, persona or style recorded, so they sit in one Old tracker (not tagged) row. Values with fewer than 8 ads are pooled into "Other"; under 5 ads only the count is shown. Spend is Meta spend on graded ads.
